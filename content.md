## Supporting the Webcast

For 13 years, the Live Webcast has connected you, our Remote Participants among the global Burner community directly to the art, dust storms, sunrises, and burns across Black Rock City. We've carried your messages to the Temple and we've connected friends and family back at home. It's a labor of love for those who can't be on playa, independently run by an all volunteer crew.

We are grateful to the Burning Man Project for covering the real foundations: credentials, meals, and camping at Media Mecca and BMIR, plus the storage ranch, heavy equipment access, power, internet, and the staff who take care of us.

Everything past that foundation, the video gear, the hardware and software, and the crew who show up early to build, stay late to tear down, and stay with OC-11 through the festivities, runs on the webcast team's own sweat and your donations.

This year's goal is an ambitious $30,000 that could get us set for at least the next 3 years. Here's where it goes:

### The Studio
OC-11, our studio and home is turning at least 25 years old and needs real love: a leaking window, the steel door hinges are famously loud and nearly frozen, rust that needs to be properly treated and not just painted over. A newer, quieter A/C and sound proofing. We've always been willing to put in the sweat to maintain OC-11, but the level of work required, and to do it right is truly beyond our capabilities. 

### Privacy First
This year we're experimenting with new tech to blur or anonymize faces in the stream in real time, so more of the community can be near the cameras without giving up their privacy, but to show you higher resolution video, we're going to need a big upgrade to the computer. This same investment leaves us room to try new things as creative video tools improve. Ensuring Consent and Respecting privacy always comes first, but there is more fun to be had.

### Getting to The Playa
It was easy when we lived in the Bay area, but much of the team is scattered across the country. Burning Man for us isn't just a party in the desert around Labor Day, it's a sweaty dusty work trip to the ranch to clean up OC-11 and prepare the gear for next year. 

Transportation isn't just real work, it's a real expense and it's only getting harder.

### The Ask
$30,000 is a complete, ideal estimate for 2026, but it doesn't have to all be cash. If your gifts do not get us our goal, gear, materials, and skills count too.<br>Email Motorbikematt@burningman.org.

### A Note on YouTube
As you know we've been trying to get off YouTube over the years through more platforms than we care to list. The past 2 years Livepeer supported us but they are no longer operating at the scale we need. Finding a real alternative costs real money that small donations won't be able to cover. 
So this year, the stream is back on YouTube. 
That might mean ads and ContentID strikes from music picked up incidentally. Honestly, landing back here again is frustrating. We know that ads aren't on all long-duration livestreams (like Friends of Big Bear Valley) but we don't know yet and will need to hear from you how the stream fares this year. 

Just know that giving you an ad-free stream in the future is part of what this support will go toward, alongside the real needs listed above and more.
