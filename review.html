Supporting the Webcast

For 13 years, the Live Webcast has connected the global Burner community directly to the art, dust storms, sunrises, and burns of Black Rock City. It is a labor of love gifted to those who cannot be on playa, created by an independent volunteer team.

We operate as an independently funded department. While the Burning Man Project generously provides essential on-playa foundations—including shipping container studio space, site power, credentials, volunteer meals, and high-speed internet with IT support staff—all specialized broadcast cameras, optics, rigging, and operational costs are sustained entirely by community donations.

24/7 Live Playa Art Window

Bringing the sights, ambient energy, and community of Black Rock City to viewers, regional groups, and Burners around the world.

Playa-Proof Cameras & Optics

High-definition robotic PTZ cameras, protective optical filters, and sealed dome enclosures engineered to withstand caustic alkaline dust.

Studio Infrastructure & Power

Container mounting masts, signal switchers, weatherized cabling, and UPS power conditioning to protect sensitive broadcast electronics.

Volunteer Operations & Sustainability

Helping sustain 13 years of volunteer technical dedication by offsetting equipment freight, secure year-round storage, and out-of-pocket expenses.
